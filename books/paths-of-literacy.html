About This Book

Paths of Literacy examines how reading, writing, and public learning shape communities across educational and cultural settings. Written for students, educators, and community practitioners, the book connects literacy theory with grounded examples from classrooms, libraries, and local publication initiatives.

The volume offers a clear framework for understanding literacy as a social practice. Each chapter balances conceptual explanation with practical reflection, making it suitable for academic courses, training programs, and independent reading.

Dr. Rania Prameswari is a literacy researcher and educator whose work focuses on reading culture, community learning, and publication-based education programs.
