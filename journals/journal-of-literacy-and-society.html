Aims and Scope

The Journal of Literacy and Society publishes research on literacy as a social, cultural, and educational practice. It welcomes empirical studies, conceptual papers, literature reviews, and community-based research.

The journal encourages submissions that connect literacy research with schools, families, libraries, digital environments, and civic learning.

Author Guidelines

  • Manuscripts should be original, unpublished, and between 5,000 and 8,000 words.
  • Use a clear academic structure with abstract, keywords, introduction, method, findings, discussion, and references where appropriate.
  • References should follow APA style. All submissions are screened for originality and reviewed by at least two reviewers.
