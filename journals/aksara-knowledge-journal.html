Aims and Scope

Aksara Knowledge Journal provides a forum for interdisciplinary work in humanities, communication, publication studies, and knowledge systems.

The journal welcomes manuscripts that examine how ideas are produced, edited, circulated, preserved, and interpreted across institutions and communities.

Author Guidelines

  • Submissions should be between 6,000 and 9,000 words and may use qualitative, interpretive, historical, or conceptual approaches.
  • Authors should explain the contribution of the manuscript to humanities, communication, or publication studies.
  • All manuscripts undergo editorial screening followed by double-anonymous peer review.
