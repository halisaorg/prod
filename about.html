About HALISA

HALISA is a publishing company focused on harmony, literacy, knowledge, authorship, and academic contribution.

Meaning

HALISA = A Hub for Academic Literacy, Ideas, and Aksara

HALISA, A Hub for Academic Literacy, Ideas, and Aksara, is built around the belief that publishing should bring balance between thoughtful language, careful knowledge, and the people who need those ideas.

Through journals, books, and academic publication services, HALISA aims to become a trusted home for authors, researchers, educators, and readers who care about clarity and contribution.

Vision

To nurture publications that elevate literacy and academic life.

HALISA envisions a publishing ecosystem where ideas are edited with care, shared with purpose, and received by communities that value learning.

Mission

To publish journals and books with integrity, warmth, and reach.

We help authors develop polished manuscripts, support scholarly communication, and create publications that invite readers into deeper knowledge.

Values

The principles behind our publishing work

Literacy

We support reading, writing, and publication practices that make ideas accessible and enduring.

Knowledge

We value careful scholarship, editorial integrity, and meaningful academic contribution.

Contribution

We publish work that strengthens scholarship, public learning, and meaningful exchange.
